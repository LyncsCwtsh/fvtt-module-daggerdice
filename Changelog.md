## 1.3.0
- NEW Codex domain inspired d12 in three variants:
  - Codex pages: a variety of blues with your choice of text colour. Designed with white in mind, but go wild
  - Codex pages (fill): black pages with writing of the colour you desire
  - Codex pages (cutout) white words on whatever page colour you wish for while keeping the light colour shift between pages
- NEW Rainbow texture: I wanted more rainbow. I added more rainbow
- NEW Pastel Rainbow theme: if you set your theme to this, the colours of your dice will change with every roll. More rainbooooww!
